World 1 is finished art and all — both brothers in five forms, the pigeons, the rats, the Pigeon King, and a Boro Park street that runs from daylight through dusk to the night of the boss fight. World 2 is three levels in: Central Avenue, Backyards, and The Pool, which is the first water in the game — Mendy swims higher, Berel walks along the bottom, and the filter is running the whole time. Boxes hold Cholent, Menorah, Lulav and Peyos; a hit costs you one form, not all of them. Press G on the map for the greybox test levels, or I to watch the opening again.
