# Worlds 2, 3 and 4 - art package

Everything for the Five Towns, the Catskills and Meah Shearim: casts, hazards,
bosses, tilesets, backdrops and goals.

**Mendy and Berel are not in here.** They are world-agnostic and already
delivered - all five forms work unchanged in every world.

Generated by `worlds234.py`, which imports `generate.py` and `scenery.py` (both
already in `tools/pixelart/`) to share their palette and renderers. Put it
beside them and run:

```bash
python tools/pixelart/worlds234.py --out assets/sprites
```

Conventions are identical to World 1: authored at 1x, never pre-scaled,
bottom-centre anchored except where noted, art frames intentionally larger than
hitboxes. Hitboxes below are what I drew to - set them in code to match, since
none of these enemies exist there yet.

**Goose** - `idle`, `hiss` (the tell), `run` (charging), `angry` (after the
first stomp), `squash`. It takes **two stomps**: the first one only makes it
angrier, which is the joke and also the mechanic.

**Dog** - `quiet` / `bark`. It can never reach the player; the bark just stuns
for a moment.

**Sprinkler** - `down` / `up`. The spray launches the player upward, so it is
also the intended route to high platforms. Hitbox applies to the head only; the
water column is a separate trigger.

**Minivan and mower** - roofs are flat and rideable. The minivan's sloped nose
should not collide.

**The Escalade** - four frames: `phase1`, `phase2`, `phase3`, `beaten`. Roof
dents deepen and the windscreen cracks as it goes. The driver is drawn in every
frame - sunglasses, phone, salad - and **never reacts**. In `phase3` the salad
is gone from her hand (she has set it in the cupholder); that is the beat where
the music drops out. In `beaten` the hazard lights are on. She is never harmed:
all damage lands on the vehicle.

**Tileset** `five_towns_tileset.png`: lawn, hedge, siding, shingle, driveway, fence, pool_water, pool_tile, deck

**Backdrops**: `bg_sky_five_towns_<v>`, `bg_far_five_towns_<v>` (tree line),
`bg_near_five_towns_<v>` (houses and lawn). Variants `day`, `dusk`.

**Mosquito swarm** - centre-anchored, two frames, alternate fast. Cannot be
stomped; outrun it or disperse it with a power-up.

**Raccoon** - the thief. On contact it takes the power-up and bolts; chase it
down and it drops it.

**Skunk** - pair with `spray.png` as a lingering cloud that blocks a path until
it clears.

**Porch step** - `ok` / `cracked`. Swap to `cracked` on landing, then drop it a
beat later.

**The bear** - `idle`, `swipe`, `hurt`. Boss-scale at 29 x 38, comparable to the
Pigeon King. It calls in raccoons when wounded.

**Tileset** `catskills_tileset.png`: dirt, mud, bungalow_wall, porch_wood, shingle, trunk, leaves, lake, lawn

**Backdrops**: `bg_sky_catskills_<v>`, `bg_far_catskills_<v>` (hills),
`bg_near_catskills_<v>` (pines). Variants `day`, `night`. The night set is for
3-3, the limited-visibility level - keep it dark and let the light radius do the
work.

## Backdrop rules - same as World 1

All layers **320 x 180**, seamless left-to-right. Scroll factors: sky 0.0,
`bg_far_*` ~0.25, `bg_near_*` ~0.5.

The far and near layers for all three worlds are **opaque along the bottom
edge**, so they can slide down as the camera climbs without exposing a
transparent cut.
